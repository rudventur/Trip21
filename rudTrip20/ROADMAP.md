# RudVentur Roadmap

Ideas and future directions for RudVentur (rudventur.com), captured as they come up.

## Marketing / Social Media

### Tunel ze światełkami (PL) — "Tunnel with lights", not "light at the end of the tunnel"

The usual cliché is "światełko w tunelu" — a single light waiting at the far end.
The idea here is the opposite: a **tunnel that is lit all the way through** —
many lights strung along its whole length, not one payoff at the exit.

Applied to social media:

- Build a **"tunnel" content format for RudVentur's social accounts**, adapted
  per platform/account (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, etc. each get their own
  version suited to that platform's format).
- The tunnel is made of a *sequence of lights* (posts/moments/beats), not a
  single destination post.
- Each light in the tunnel needs **its own multiple reasons** to exist — i.e.
  every post/beat in the sequence should be independently justified (why post
  it, what it hooks into, why it matters on its own), not just a filler step
  on the way to a big reveal.
- Goal: continuous engagement throughout the journey/story, mirroring
  RudVentur's own "the trip is the point" spirit — not a slow build to one
  climax.

Open questions for later refinement:
- How many "lights" per tunnel, and how are they paced per platform?
- How do we generate/curate the "multiple reasons" behind each light
  (content pillars, campaign tie-ins, user-generated content, etc.)?
- Can this tie into the in-app trip results (each stop on a route = one
  light in the tunnel)?
